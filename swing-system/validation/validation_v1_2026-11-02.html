Honest caveats

  • SURVIVORSHIP BIAS: the universe is rebuilt each month from official NSE data (including stocks later delisted), but free price history (Yahoo) exists only for stocks still listed today. Stocks that were in the universe but later delisted, merged or renamed are MISSING from the backtest. Those are often the losers, so results are probably flattered. Coverage is reported above.
  • DATA QUALITY: prices come from Yahoo Finance and are cross-checked against NSE official closing prices; stocks that disagree or have unexplained jumps are quarantined (excluded). Yahoo data still contains undetected errors, and demergers are not adjusted.
  • FUNDAMENTALS are NOT part of this backtest (free point-in-time history does not exist for 10+ years). The live quality screen can only remove tips; its effect is untested.
  • RESULTS DATES used in tests are actual historical dates; in reality a date is known only a few days in advance.
  • UNCERTAINTY: a few hundred trades over ~12 years is a small sample. Past performance - even when honestly tested - does not predict future returns. Market regimes change.
  • COSTS: Zerodha delivery charges (STT 0.1% each side, exchange, SEBI, GST, stamp duty, DP Rs 15.34 per sell) + 0.15% slippage per side are included. Income tax on gains (STCG) is NOT included.
